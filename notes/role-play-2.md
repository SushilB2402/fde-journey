## Evaluation Summary & Misses

### Against the Three Rules
1. **Talk about their life, not your idea:** Mostly met. Opener was strong, though "real-time" slipped in and the prototype pitch re-introduced product framing.
2. **Ask about specifics in the past:** Met for most of the call (last occurrence, frequency, decision unlocked).
3. **Talk less, listen more:** Partly met (~43% of words spoken). Longer turns occurred near the end during summarizing and proposing.

### Key Misses
1. **The Discrepancy Signal:** Ignored Dana’s note that the booking export and cardiology spreadsheet "never quite match." Needs follow-up regarding data trust.
2. **Premature Dashboard Lock-in:** Dana stated "I'm not sure it's a dashboard problem," which was unaddressed. A scheduled report might suffice.
3. **Asking Dana about Priya's Process:** Asked second-hand technical details instead of asking who owns the data pipeline.
4. **Unconfirmed Success Metric:** Declared the metric "crystal clear" without asking Dana to confirm a measurable threshold.
5. **No Advancement / One-Sided Commitments:** The FDE committed to doing work (sending outline, notes) while Dana committed to nothing concrete ("I'll try to read it").
