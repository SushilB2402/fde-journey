# FDE Discovery Questions Framework

These 10 questions translate the principles of *The Mom Test* into a structured discovery call workflow for an Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE). They are designed to elicit concrete past behavior, quantify problem severity, and clarify stakeholders and system access.

---

### 1. "What are the main things you’re trying to fix or improve in the department right now?"
* **Focus:** Priority and Context
* **Objective:** Establish macro-level goals and gauge where this problem ranks relative to other departmental fires before narrowing in on specifics.

### 2. "Walk me through the last time you needed to know how many appointments were missed."
* **Focus:** Last Specific Occurrence
* **Objective:** Anchor the conversation on an actual past instance rather than an idealized process or future hypothetical.

### 3. "What happened the last time those numbers were late or wrong?"
* **Focus:** Step-by-Step Walkthrough
* **Objective:** Uncover the cascade of operational friction, manual data collection errors, or structural bottlenecks across systems.

### 4. "How many times did that come up last month, and roughly how much time did it take?"
* **Focus:** Frequency and Cost
* **Objective:** Quantify the true scale and repetition of the pain point with hard numbers.

### 5. "How do you get that information today? Who pulls it, and with what tools?"
* **Focus:** Current Process and Data Ownership
* **Objective:** Map the technical workflow, data schemas, software stack, and key personnel currently responsible for the data.

### 6. "What have you tried so far to improve it, and what happened?"
* **Focus:** Existing Attempts
* **Objective:** Evaluate past attempted solutions, existing internal build failures, or abandoned vendor integrations to understand existing constraints.

### 7. "What do you do with that number once you have it? What decision does it feed?"
* **Focus:** Digging Beneath the Feature / Dashboard Request
* **Objective:** Move past surface-level requests (e.g., *"we need a dashboard with 12 charts"*) to identify the fundamental operational decision or business outcome required.

### 8. "What did the last delay or error cost you in time, money, or patient impact?"
* **Focus:** Past Consequences
* **Objective:** Surface explicit past losses or risk factors to confirm if the problem is severe enough to justify engineering resources.

### 9. "Who else is affected by this, and who should I talk to next?"
* **Focus:** Stakeholder Mapping
* **Objective:** Identify downstream teams, end users, database administrators, and budget owners who need to be aligned.

### 10. "If I came back with a small first version, what would you need to provide to try it: data access, an introduction, some of your team’s time?"
* **Focus:** Commitment and Next Steps
* **Objective:** Test buy-in and real skin in the game by securing tangible commitments (data access, time, intro to decision-makers) rather than polite praise.
